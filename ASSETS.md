# Assets

- Cottage, sea, sailboat, tree, garden flowers, and shell: original inline SVG artwork created for this prototype, in `src/main.js`.
- Interface: original HTML/CSS, in `src/style.css`.
- Type: Fraunces and DM Sans loaded from Google Fonts, distributed under the SIL Open Font License; system fallbacks are provided.
- Sound: original synthesized chime and an original 16-bar instrumental loop in `src/music.js`, using Web Audio oscillators; no recorded audio or third-party music.
- No third-party game art or raster image-generation assets.
